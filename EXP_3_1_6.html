Microwave Fabry–Perot Interferometer

Electric field in a cavity with partially transmitting mirrors


Mirror separation (in wavelengths): 2.00

Intracavity forward-wave amplitude buildup: 1.58×

At resonance (L = nλ/2), the electric field has minima at both mirrors. These approach exact nodes as mirror reflectivity approaches 100%. Here the mirrors reflect 60% of the incident power, so the minima are not exact nodes.

Black: incident field on the left, total field inside the cavity, and transmitted field on the right. Dashed red: reflected field. Each blue bar represents an idealized thin, lossless mirror with a phase-reversing reflection, not a perfectly conducting wall.
